Flood susceptibility maps are judged by how well they rank the ground that floods, yet unlike forecasts, which are scored against persistence, they are rarely compared with the simplest alternative, the record of where floods have already been. This study makes that comparison for infrastructure mapped in OpenStreetMap in Bangladesh, with flood extents mapped from Sentinel-1 radar as labels and reference and every model scored on 10\,km blocks held out from training. Each flood year was predicted from the years before it, giving \PoolTests{} tests in three study regions and four independent validation regions. The record's lead over a gradient-boosted terrain model fell as the share of a flood reaching ground no earlier flood had reached rose (Spearman $\rho=\PoolRho{}$, $p\PoolRhoP{}$), whatever the length of the record, and the two were level when about a third of flooded assets stood on such new ground. In Rangpur and Rajshahi, where only \RRRollTwentyFourNewGround{}\,\% of the 2024 river flooding reached new ground, the record ranked it at \RRTempPastAUC{} against \RRTempAUC{} for the model and kept its lead with masks re-mapped from a single satellite, with other training targets and with 40\,km blocks, while on the cyclone-struck coast, where most flooding reached new ground, the model led. A global hydrodynamic hazard map was no stronger a rival than the model, and the model given the record as a feature beat both of its parts in \CombinedAheadBoth{} of \CombinedTests{} tests, all riverine. Radar-observed extents also made better training labels than terrain thresholds, by \RRLabelGap{}, \SylhetLabelGap{} and \CoastalLabelGap{} in AUC on held-out areas. The radar masks agreed only moderately with an independent MODIS map, so every score measures agreement with flooding as radar sees it. Where a radar flood history exists, it can serve as a free planning map wherever floods return to the same ground, a susceptibility model earns its place mainly where floods reach new ground, and susceptibility studies should report the record as a baseline and score their models by the share of its ranking error they remove.
